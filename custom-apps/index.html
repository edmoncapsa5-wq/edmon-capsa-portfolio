Focused by design

Strong custom software starts with saying no to the wrong scope. These boundaries keep small projects affordable and deliverable.

Good fit

  • Private tools and micro-apps
  • Personal or relationship apps
  • Client portals and trackers
  • Focused internal workflows
  • Calculators, records, dashboards

Needs separate scoping

  • Instant clones of Uber, Facebook, etc.
  • Regulated banking or medical infrastructure
  • Guaranteed app-store approval
  • Unlimited revisions
  • “Free hosting forever” commitments

Complex or high-risk projects may be quoted separately or declined.

FAQ

What most people need to know before submitting an idea.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe the outcome, problem, or experience you want. Technical decisions are part of scoping.

Do we need a video call?

No, not by default. Forms, chat, and email are enough for many focused projects.

Can you build for phones?

Yes. Responsive web apps/PWAs are the usual default because they work on phones and computers. Native Android/iOS is quoted when it is actually necessary.

Can it be private or login-protected?

Yes, when authentication and private data access are part of the agreed scope.

Do you build business apps only?

No. Personal, couple, family, group, professional, creator, and business apps are all within the service when the scope is appropriate.

What if I don’t know what features I need?

Describe the problem and the current process. We can propose the smallest useful feature set.

What costs are not included?

Hosting, domains, paid APIs, app-store fees, and third-party services are separate unless explicitly included in the quote.
