# Case Studies

These are sanitized portfolio examples designed to show how I approach common Operations & Administrative VA work without exposing client-sensitive information.

## Available Case Studies

### [CRM Data Cleanup](./crm-data-cleanup.md)
Shows how I separate clean records from ambiguous exceptions, protect customer identity, and avoid unsafe imports.

### [SOP & Process Control](./sop-process-control.md)
Shows how I turn an operator-dependent recurring task into a documented, transferable process with decision points and stop conditions.

### [Accounting Document Control](./accounting-document-control.md)
Shows how I review and organize transaction documents, prepare bookkeeping inputs, isolate exceptions, and preserve reviewer authority.

---

## What These Samples Are Intended to Prove

- I can organize messy operational work into controlled steps.
- I can distinguish routine work from decisions requiring authority.
- I can document exceptions rather than hide them.
- I can communicate status in a way that helps the client decide what happens next.
- I can use AI and automation as tools without treating their output as unquestionable.

Additional samples will be added for research, spreadsheet work, project coordination, and workflow automation.
