### Why this example matters

The published policy is already sensible: extra requests should be separately quoted. **This does not demonstrate that the studio needs another tool.** It simply shows the narrow area a customized approval-to-billing log *might* cover when pricing, approval and invoicing are coordinated manually.

1. Review actual project quotation and whether request is in scope.
2. Quote the correct additional work and schedule impact.
3. Capture explicit acceptance and an approval reference.
4. Release work and notify the delivery owner.
5. Confirm the extra was invoiced only when an invoice reference exists.
