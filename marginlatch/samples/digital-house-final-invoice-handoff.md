# From an extra website request to the final invoice — illustrative one-page handoff

**Independent fictional example for evaluating MarginLatch. Not prepared for, commissioned by, endorsed by, or based on private information from Digital House MD Ltd. No actual client, project, approval or unpaid work is asserted.**

### Why this example

[Digital House's publicly available terms, last updated 30 September 2026](https://www.digitalhousemd.co.uk/terms-and-conditions) say website-design quotes ordinarily include up to three feedback/revision rounds (unless the proposal differs), and that extra work beyond the original scope can be charged or added to the final invoice. Those are **published commercial rules**, not evidence that its internal systems are inadequate.

### Fictional example: the fourth revision round

| Checkpoint | Sample entry / rule |
| --- | --- |
| Project baseline | Agreed website scope, quote and **three** included revision rounds (illustrative contract assumption) |
| Incoming request | Client asks for another consolidated design adjustment after the third included round |
| First classification | Check whether this is a chargeable extra or a correction needed because the work failed the agreed brief |
| Proposed price | **Not set.** The studio decides an appropriate fee under its specific quote and terms |
| Schedule impact | **Not set.** Delivery owner reviews and specifies any change before client approval |
| Client approval | **PENDING.** No chargeable extra work is authorised merely by receiving the request |
| Commercial discretion | Account owner may propose **no-charge goodwill treatment**, recorded with an internal authoriser and reason |
| Finance handoff | Only an authorised, chargeable addition proceeds to billing with a valid approval/evidence link |
| Invoice check | Confirm the addition appears on the actual invoice and record its reference; a waived or excluded item remains non-billable |

**Two different internal routes, both valid:**

1. **Chargeable:** identify out-of-scope work → approve an estimate internally → communicate fee and timing → obtain a real client acceptance → carry out authorised work → verify the line on the final invoice → mark invoiced.
2. **Waived as goodwill:** identify extra work → obtain internal waiver authorisation and note the reason → tell the delivery team that the work is deliberately no-charge → **exclude the amount from approved and invoiced revenue totals**. No fictitious "recovered fee" is claimed.

**If it is a correction to the studio's own error,** it must not be relabelled chargeable simply to increase a report. The particular customer agreement governs the correct treatment.

### Example email for a genuinely chargeable fourth round

We'll confirm a separate estimate and any timing impact before scheduling this work. Please don't treat this as approved or booked until we've agreed those details in writing.
