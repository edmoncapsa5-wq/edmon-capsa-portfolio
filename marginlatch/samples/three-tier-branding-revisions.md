# Example: recording extra branding revisions without losing the billing handoff

> **Independent fictional demonstration** by MarginLatch. **Not commissioned, endorsed or used by Big Yellow Marketing Communications Ltd.** We have not reviewed any of its private processes or invoices, and do not assert that it has an admin or billing problem.

## What is publicly documented

Big Yellow Marketing Communications lists three logo/branding tiers with different included refinement allowances and charges for additional rounds:

| Public package | Included rounds | Public extra-work rules |
|---|---:|---|
| Essential / Launch Logo | 2 | Additional revisions listed at £30–£60 + VAT per round |
| Professional / Growth Brand | 3 | Additional revisions listed at £30–£60 + VAT per round |
| Premium / Complete Brand Identity | 4 | Additional work described as hourly or per round |

The company also distinguishes minor revisions from a new concept direction and treats significant brief changes as a new phase. **Source:** [public graphic design packages](https://www.bigyell.co.uk/graphic-design/), consulted October 2026.

## One hypothetical request, four checkpoints

**Scenario only:** a fictional Essential logo client has completed both included revision rounds and requests one further minor refinement round. The studio proposes an **illustrative £45 + VAT** for the third round, within the publicly listed £30–£60 range. This figure is **not an actual quote from the agency or a promise that it would charge that amount.**

| Checkpoint | Fictional record | What the system should do |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Scope** | Package: Essential; 2 of 2 included rounds used; request: third minor revision | Flag that the agreed revision allowance must be checked against the contract |
| **2. Quote** | Proposed: £45 + VAT; turnaround impact: to be confirmed | Mark as **Quoted / Awaiting approval**; do not treat as money earned or approved |
| **3. Written approval** | Approval reference: **not yet received** | Keep approved-value total at **£0** and do not start chargeable work |
| **4. Invoice handoff** | Invoice reference: **none** | Keep invoiced-value total at **£0** and prompt the billing owner after actual approval/delivery |

**Example email for a hypothetical client:**

> Thanks for the additional refinements. Your Essential logo package includes two revision rounds, which have been used in this example. We can quote a further minor revision round at £45 + VAT, with the delivery timing to be confirmed. Would you like to approve the extra fee and revised timing before we schedule it?

**Control rule:** a client reply must genuinely accept the final fee and timing; typing “approved” or an invoice reference into the file is not independent proof. Invoices, permissions and enforceable fees remain governed by the agency's own agreement.

## Why this is a workflow, not just a blank template

If no one owns the handoff, an additional round can be discussed by a designer and completed without the person responsible for billing seeing the approved addition. A studio-specific setup names the internal scope checker, pricing authority, client-contact owner and billing owner, then adapts the tracker and email templates to those responsibilities.
