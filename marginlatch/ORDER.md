## Target turnaround: two business days, once agreed

The seller proposes handoff within **two business days** of cleared payment and complete intake and confirms an actual date/time in the order email. This is not an instant download and not a pre-committed delivery date before agreement.

## Five buyer-verifiable acceptance checks

1. The configured Excel file opens without a repair prompt and has **no fictional transactions mixed into live totals**.
2. The agreed service tiers, currency, revision limits and role handoffs are correctly reflected.
3. An extra request **is not included as approved** in the tracker/dashboard without a recorded written client-approval reference. A deliberate **Waived** decision must instead cite an internal owner decision and appears only as estimated goodwill value.
4. An extra request **is not included as invoiced** without a valid recorded client approval reference **and** invoice reference; complimentary/waived work is not booked as invoiced.
5. The branded editable approval form, **five** messages, **one-page SOP**, worked demonstration and quickstart are included and readable.

The buyer's team must check the **authenticity** of the underlying client agreement, approval and invoice. File checks cannot do that.

## Remedy and refund

- If we cannot deliver the mutually agreed paid package, the setup fee is refunded through a previously agreed and available method.
- A buyer may report a material failure against the five acceptance checks within **seven calendar days of delivery**. We will remedy that in-scope defect without extra charge within **three business days after the buyer provides enough detail** to identify it.
- If we cannot remedy it, we refund the setup fee. Transfer/FX charges and refund processing are disclosed in the individual order before collection; this commitment does not restrict mandatory consumer or business rights.
- The one in-scope revision round is separate from defect correction. It does not create unlimited custom development.
