Before you quote

Know if the job is worth taking before you price it.

Enter the expected costs and your target margin. This tool calculates the minimum quote needed to protect that margin. It deliberately does not include CRM, invoicing, payroll, or accounting.

Key distinction: margin and markup are not the same. If a job costs $1,000 and you want a 25% gross margin, the quote is $1,333.33—not $1,250.
